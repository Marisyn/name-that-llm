## Content

- I let Claude Code run the experiments and kept the decisions to myself; the most useful habit was asking "is that really true?" (it caught guesses and stale leaderboard numbers more than once).
- A risk register and a plan-vs-actual log helped: my top-ranked risk, the public notebook line moving up, happened the next day.
- What didn't work: local gains that vanished on the LB, and external data that lowered CV because its formatting doesn't match the competition texts.
