## Content

Many of us worry that this competition rewards paid generation. As a first-timer, I also saw how much open data and public notebooks helped me learn. I wrote up both sides and five small proposals: hosts clarify the rules on generated data and share the formatting protocol; participants publish generated data with protocol and license, list data dependencies in notebooks, and report a competition-data-only score when possible.

The full writeup is on my profile (Writeups tab): "Pay to Win, or Learn to Win? A Beginner's View of External Data in Name That LLM". Feedback welcome, especially from people who generated data themselves.
