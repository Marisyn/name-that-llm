## Content

To make grouped-CV results comparable, I published fold assignments for train.csv: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshitsama/name-that-llm-grouped-cv-folds . Columns: id, prompt_id, grouped_fold_seed0/1/2 (all answers to a prompt in one fold, like the unseen-prompt test set) and stratified_fold_seed0/1/2. No text and no labels, so it adds no competition data; join on id with your own train.csv. Measured with my baseline (TF-IDF + LinearSVC): grouped 0.3126, stratified 0.1315, public LB 0.308. Write-up: https://www.kaggle.com/code/harshitsama/name-that-llm-grouped-cv-beats-stratified-cv . If your grouped CV and LB disagree, tell me; I would like to compare.
