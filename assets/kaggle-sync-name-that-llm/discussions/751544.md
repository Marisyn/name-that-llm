## Content

Hi, grouping a bunch of questions that have come up across various threads so there is one place for a consolidated response. Would really help with planning the next few weeks.

1. On publicly shared datasets of passages generated via vendor APIs. Examples include seyitkaangunes/ntl-extra-api-passages, lucasboesen/ntl-free-proxy-generations, and olegzholobov/name-that-llm-response-corpus. These were uploaded under CC licenses by other competitors who paid the API costs themselves, and are now freely available to every participant. Is using them for training compliant with the External Data clause?

2. If a participant generates their own passages via vendor APIs and publishes the resulting dataset on Kaggle under a permissive license before the final deadline, is that compliant too? From the moment of publication it meets the "publicly available free to all" test.

3. Section 6 mentions a Reasonableness Standard around cost. The example of a Gemini Advanced subscription suggests the threshold is low. Could you give a rough sense of what dollar range of API usage over the full competition period you would consider acceptable? Even an order of magnitude would help a lot.

4. On forking. Many of the top public kernels are being copied wholesale by other competitors and submitted with little or no modification. This seems to be universally accepted practice but is not addressed explicitly in the rules. Can you confirm it is allowed?

5. On using test predictions in training. If predictions on the hidden test during Stage 2 are used as additional training labels in a second training pass before final inference, does that violate anything? Want to understand whether self-training patterns like this are compliant for this competition.

6. On team mergers. After a successful merger before the January 6 deadline, can team members share private datasets, private kernels, and prediction files among themselves, or does the private sharing restriction still apply within the merged team?
