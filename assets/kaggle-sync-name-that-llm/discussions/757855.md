Some findings that may help others:
- Random CV is pessimistic here (0.104 vs 0.315 grouped by prompt): the other 17 texts of the same prompt sit in train and the topic points to the wrong models.
- Hungarian assignment within prompt groups gave +0.10 CV but +0.002 LB. Simulating unequal per-model counts in validation, n/18 grouping drops to ARI 0.61; capped agglomerative grouping stays at 0.995, but the gain still mostly vanishes on LB.
- Typographic negative invariants mined from train (each rule: chance level < 1e-4): unspaced em dash never appears in gemini-3.1/3.6/3.8 (1,052 texts from others); straight apostrophe never in gpt-5.6-sol (835 texts); straight double quotes never in gpt-5.6-luna/sol/terra, gpt-6-astra, qwen3.8-max, gemini-3.7.
