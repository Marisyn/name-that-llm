## Content

Hi Kaggle team,

Rule 2.6 permits external data that is publicly available at no cost to all participants or that meets the Reasonableness Standard. However, it is unclear whether passages generated by calling the APIs of the 18 candidate models listed on the competition’s Evaluation page qualify as permitted external data.

Could you please clarify the following?

Data generation: Is self-generated training data produced using the candidate models’ APIs permitted under Rule 2.6?

Reasonableness Standard: If permitted, is there a spending limit or other cost threshold that teams must follow?

Public availability: Would publishing the generated passages as a public dataset for all participants before using them affect their eligibility?

Our experiments suggest that the existing public pool of matched passages is approaching saturation at approximately 86,000 unique rows. We have observed an improvement of roughly 0.03 in cross-validation balanced accuracy with each doubling of matched training data. Clarification is therefore important for understanding the rules and ensuring a fair competition.

We will not generate or use API-generated training data until we receive clarification.

Thank you for your time and guidance.
