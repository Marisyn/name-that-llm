## Content

I collected an external response corpus for **Name That LLM**, organized as:

`id` · `prompt` · **18 model-name columns**

[**Dataset**](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olegzholobov/name-that-llm-response-corpus) · [**Overview notebook**](https://www.kaggle.com/code/olegzholobov/name-that-llm-wide-data-overview) · [**Seaborn NLP explorer**](https://www.kaggle.com/code/olegzholobov/name-that-llm-seaborn-nlp-explorer)

| External view | Unique responses | Wide rows |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full corpus | **135,171** | 70,980 |
| 40–250-word candidates | **24,015** | 13,642 |

**1,902 prompt groups have answers from all 18 models.** Source URLs, model-label evidence and quality flags are preserved. The latest additions include Artificial Analysis, Novigens, APIFlow, JetBrains and RISE releases.

**Overlap check:** no exact or normalized matches with 2,699 original training answers, and no complete original answer found inside a longer external response. Candidate-based similarity checks also found no matches at 90%; this does not rule out every paraphrase.

The notebooks keep original and external data separate. Original data loads through the competition attachment; missing prompt wording remains null. The NLP notebook has nine charts covering length, vocabulary, punctuation and prompt coverage.

**Start with** `external_short_wide.parquet`. Full data includes code, categorical and multimodal answers. A separate Luna quarantine has weaker labels and is excluded from default analysis.

For validation, group by prompt and keep duplicate responses together. Shared prompts may have different system/history settings. Source licenses vary; see provenance and README.
