## Content

A quick pointer for anyone who hasn't seen it: [ntl-extra-api-passages](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/seyitkaangunes/ntl-extra-api-passages) has ~5,000 labelled paragraphs from the 5 Claude labels.

- The prompts are 1,000 new ones in the competition's style.
- The passages were generated through the official API.
- No competition text is included.

**What seems to work:** use it to pick which Claude, not to train the 18-way classifier.

1. Fit a small within-Claude model on the train Claude rows plus these passages.
2. Apply it only to rows your blend already predicts as Claude.

This is the "Claude gate" in @zbigniewkirchner's public notebook, and it gave him a clear LB gain. Dropping the rows straight into the 18-class training tends to hurt the other classes.

Thanks for the dataset and the shout-out! It's the cleanest extra data in this competition so far. Two more ways to use it, if anyone wants to go further:

Inside a LoRA: I added a 5-way Claude head to a Qwen3-8B LoRA and trained it on the original Claude rows plus your passages, while the 18-way head sees only original data. On the public LoRA's own validation fold it gives 0.484 vs 0.456 (plain), and 0.493 with the Claude head used as a gate. Weights: [ntl-qwen3-8b-lora-claude-head](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zbigniewkirchner/ntl-qwen3-8b-lora-claude-head)

In a hierarchy (lab → line → version, extra data only below the lab level): +2.4 pts for a plain CPU stylegram model, with a bootstrap CI that doesn't cross zero. [Notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/zbigniewkirchner/extra-data-hierarchical-classification-2-4-pts)
